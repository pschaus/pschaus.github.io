<a href="https://bitbucket.org/oscarlib/oscar/wiki/Home"  target="_blank">OscaR</a> is a Scala toolkit for solving Operations Research problems. The techniques currently available in OscaR are:

<ul>
<li>Constraint Programming</li>
<li>Constrained Based Local Search</li>
<li>Discrete Event Simulation</li>
<li>Derivative Free Optimization</li>
<li>Visualization</li>
</ul>

I mostly develop Constraint Programming aspects of OscaR.
